About Oltrix

A small institute with one narrow focus.

We teach data, analytics and digital marketing — and nothing else. That focus is the reason our mentors can review your work closely instead of skimming it.

Who we are

Built for people on a deadline.

Oltrix Education is a career-focused training institute based in Mangalore. Most of our students are graduates starting out, early-career professionals, or people leaving an unrelated field for good.

What they share is a deadline: a first job, a promotion, or an interview next month they need to be ready for. That shapes everything — the size of a batch, the amount of project work, and the fact that our mentors still hold day jobs in the industry.

We deliberately keep the catalog small. Six tracks, each with a defined outcome, is easier to teach well than thirty courses and a catalogue of recordings. If a course does not lead somewhere, we would rather not sell it.

Our mission

Give people the skills, the proof and the confidence to be taken seriously in a data career.

Clarity over jargon

If a concept cannot be explained plainly, we have not taught it yet. Buzzwords are not a curriculum.

Practice over theory

Every module produces a deliverable. Understanding without output is a hobby, not a qualification.

Honest about outcomes

We will tell you when a track is the wrong fit, and we do not promise jobs we cannot control.

Small enough to coach

A cohort you can be seen in. Feedback is specific, personal and uncomfortable in the useful way.

How we teach

Live, reviewed, and repeated until it sticks.

Three things define a week at Oltrix: a live session, a piece of work, and a review of that work.

See the tracks
01

Live sessions, recorded

Classes run live so you can interrupt and ask the stupid question — usually the one half the room needed answered. Recordings stay available for revision.

02

Work reviewed by a practitioner

Not a checklist. A person who does this for a living reads your notebook, dashboard or campaign and tells you what a hiring manager would say.

03

A capstone that is actually yours

You choose the problem, within a scope we help you set. The result is a project you can defend line by line in an interview.

04

Career lab at the end

Résumé, portfolio, LinkedIn and mock interviews — run as a block, so you leave applying rather than "planning to apply".

Who it's for

Three students we see most often.

Final-year students

Graduating into a market that wants experience you do not have yet. A portfolio closes part of that gap before your first interview.

Working professionals

Already in a job, stuck one skill short of the role you want. Part-time batches are built to sit alongside work.

Career switchers

Moving from a non-technical field and needing a credible story, not just a certificate. We start you where you actually are.
