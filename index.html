Questions

Before you enrol.

Still unsure? A five-minute call usually settles it.

Talk to us
Do I need coding experience?
For Data Science, Power BI, SQL, Business Analytics and Digital Marketing — no. Each opens with the foundations it needs. Machine Learning & AI assumes basic Python, which the Data Science track covers.
Online or classroom?
Sessions run live, with recordings you can revisit. Students in and around Perambra can attend in person; everyone else joins online. Tell us your preference when you enquire and we'll confirm the current batch format.
How long does a track take?
Most tracks run 6–16 weeks depending on the course, with one to two sessions a week plus project work in between. Part-time batches are designed to sit alongside a job or college.
What does it cost?
Fees differ by track and batch format, so we quote them on enquiry rather than list a number that may be out of date. Ask about instalments when you call.
Do you guarantee a job?
No — and be careful with anyone who does. We give you the portfolio, interview practice and referrals. The offer depends on your work and the market.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes, on completing the capstone project for your track. The certificate is the small print; the portfolio is the point.
